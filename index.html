What is the Digestive System?

The digestive system is a group of organs that work together to break down food into nutrients — vitamins, minerals, carbohydrates, fats, proteins and gases — that the body can use for energy, growth, and cell repair.

Ingestion — Taking food into the mouth.
2

3
Absorption — Nutrients absorbed in the small intestine.
4
Assimilation — Body uses the nutrients.
5
Egestion — Waste removal.
Interesting Facts
• The small intestine is about 6 meters long in adults.
• The stomach lining makes gastric acid (hydrochloric acid) — very strong!
• Total digestion time for a meal can be ~24–72 hours depending on what you eat.
